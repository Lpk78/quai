STEP 1 — CREATE CLEAN REFERENCE IMAGES
Before video, create or upload:
A. Operator turnaround: front / 3-4 / side / back.
B. Operator action sheet: scanning / speaking / walking / pointing.
C. Forklift turnaround.
D. Truck: side / rear closed / rear open / cargo interior.
E. Box family.
F. Warehouse wide establishing frame.

Do not start the final film until these references feel consistent.

STEP 2 — ON HIGGSFIELD
Open Create Video / Image-to-Video.
Upload the relevant reference image for the shot.
When the model supports References / Elements, add the operator reference and the object/environment reference.
Keep Elements enabled where available.
Use 16:9, 5 seconds for first tests, 1080p when available.
Do NOT ask the video model to render the QUAI logo or typography. Add them later in editing.

STEP 3 — STYLE BLOCK TO PASTE INTO EVERY SHOT
Bright premium miniature diorama of a modern logistics warehouse, realistic small-scale figures
like premium painted vinyl toys, rounded simple friendly faces, matte tactile materials, cinematic
tilt-shift macro photography, shallow depth of field, soft morning daylight, pale warm-grey floor,
warm kraft cardboard boxes, navy structural details, safety orange accents, clean cheerful precise
engineering aesthetic. Keep the exact same operator, outfit, forklift, truck, boxes and warehouse
design as the uploaded references. No brand logos, no text, no cyberpunk, no neon, no holograms,
no generic AI gradients, no LEGO-like/blocky figures.

STEP 4 — FILM SHOTS
01 Establishing: slow push-in over the logistics hub. Parcels move on conveyor; operator beside them;
forklift and open truck visible. Calm physical miniature motion.
02 Scan: medium 3/4 shot. Same operator scans one kraft box with the same dark smartphone.
03 Speak: same operator raises phone slightly and speaks naturally. Gentle push-in.
04 Understand: keep camera stable; operator watches while boxes are visually organized by physical
placement cues. No floating UI.
05 Plan: top-down 3/4 truck interior. One specific orange-highlighted box is clearly next.
06 Load: forklift lifts that exact box, moves slowly, places it precisely in the open truck.
07 Exception: operator notices one damaged/missing parcel; focused expression, checks phone.
08 Recompute: stable camera. Only affected boxes shift to new positions; calm precise motion.
09 Delivery: rear truck doors open at a destination; the correct stop's boxes are immediately accessible.
10 End: truck leaves the miniature depot toward the town. Leave clean negative space for logo in post.

STEP 5 — CONSISTENCY RULE
For every new shot, reference the SAME operator sheet and whichever recurring object is visible.
Do not regenerate the character from text alone after shot 1.

STEP 6 — VERTICAL VERSION
First make the master in 16:9.
Keep operator and action in the central 45% of frame.
Then create/reframe a 9:16 version. Do not independently redesign the vertical shot unless necessary.

STEP 7 — EDIT
Add QUAI logo and slogan only after video generation.
